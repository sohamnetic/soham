Placement Reminder

A personal tool that turns college placement emails into Telegram reminders.

What it does

  • Reads (read-only) the owner's own Gmail inbox for placement drive announcements from the college placement cell.
  • Extracts the company name, pay details, eligibility criteria and registration deadline.
  • Sends reminders to the owner's own Telegram account until they register, and alerts them when a company they applied to sends an email.

Who it is for

This app is used only by its developer for personal use. It is not offered to the public.
